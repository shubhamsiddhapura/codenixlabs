Free AI Readiness Checker

We also run a free tool that checks whether AI assistants — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity — can actually read your website. It reads your robots.txt, then requests your homepage as each named AI crawler to see what your server really does, checks your structured data and page markup, and gives you the code to fix whatever is broken. It publishes the scoring weights it used, so the number can be audited rather than trusted. Available at codenixlabs.com/ai-readiness.
